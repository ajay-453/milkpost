Questions farmers ask

Do I need internet in the barn?

MilkPost runs on your own server or a €4/month VPS. Pages are tiny and server-rendered, so they load even on weak barn signal.

Where does my data live?

On your machine, in one SQLite file. Nothing is sent anywhere. Backup = copy the file. Every table exports to CSV.

Does it know my drug withholding rules?

You enter the withholding days from the drug label or your vet — MilkPost computes the clear-date and alerts until it passes. It deliberately doesn't guess rules that vary by country and product.

Is there a login?

Not yet — V1 is single-farm and should run on your LAN or behind a VPN like Tailscale. Accounts are the first item on the roadmap.
